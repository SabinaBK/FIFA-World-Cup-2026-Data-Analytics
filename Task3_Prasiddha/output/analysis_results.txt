FIFA WORLD CUP 2026 PASSING ANALYSIS
====================================

RESEARCH QUESTION
-----------------
Is the average number of completed passes significantly different between FIFA World Cup 2026 players with passing accuracy of 90% or greater and players with passing accuracy below 90%?

HYPOTHESES
----------
H0: The mean number of completed passes is the same for players with passing accuracy >= 90% and players with passing accuracy < 90%.
H1: The mean number of completed passes is different between players with passing accuracy >= 90% and players with passing accuracy < 90%.

WELCH TWO-SAMPLE T-TEST
-----------------------
T-statistic: 4.4971
P-value: 0.000036
Significance Level (alpha): 0.05

DECISION
--------
Reject the null hypothesis (H0).

CONCLUSION
----------
There is statistically significant evidence that the average number of completed passes differs between FIFA World Cup 2026 players with passing accuracy of 90% or greater and players with passing accuracy below 90%.
The >=90% group completed an average of 294.35 passes, compared with 200.60 passes for the <90% group.
The estimated mean difference was 93.75 passes, with a 95% confidence interval from 51.96 to 135.54 passes.
